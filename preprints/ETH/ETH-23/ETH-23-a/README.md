# ETH-23-a · Moral Anachronism and Reasonable Knowledge

- **Family:** ETH-23 — May we judge past people by present standards?
- **Type:** principal
- **Discipline:** Normative and Applied Ethics
- **Estimated length:** 5 pages
- **Formalized:** no

## Main result

Yes, with allowance for epistemic distance: past agents are blameworthy only for what they could reasonably have known.
