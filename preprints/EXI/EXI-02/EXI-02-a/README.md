# EXI-02-a · Death Is Bad for the One Who Dies

- **Family:** EXI-02 — Is death bad for the one who dies?
- **Type:** principal
- **Discipline:** Meaning, Death and the Absurd
- **Estimated length:** 6 pages
- **Formalized:** no

## Main result

Yes. Death deprives the one who dies of goods, and Epicurus's argument fails because deprivation does not require a subject at the time of loss.
