# REL-12-a · Petitionary Prayer as Non-Causal Difference-Making

- **Family:** REL-12 — Does petitionary prayer make a difference?
- **Type:** principal
- **Discipline:** Philosophy of Religion
- **Estimated length:** 4 pages
- **Formalized:** no

## Main result

Yes, without causal efficacy: an outcome may be granted because it was requested, even when it would otherwise have occurred.
