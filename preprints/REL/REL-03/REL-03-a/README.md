# REL-03-a · Divine Hiddenness as a Signalling Equilibrium

- **Family:** REL-03 — Is divine hiddenness evidence against God?
- **Type:** principal
- **Discipline:** Philosophy of Religion
- **Estimated length:** 7 pages
- **Formalized:** yes, see `lean/Philosophy/REL/REL_03.lean`

## Main result

No. In the signalling game between God and free creatures, unambiguous revelation would constrain the freedom of response; hiddenness is the equilibrium a perfectly loving God would choose.
