# Computed part of REL-01-c

Python 3, standard library only. Regenerate (about two minutes), from this directory:

    python3 generate.py

Files:

- `models.py`: finite second-order Kripke models (worlds, accessibility, constant domain, a Boolean
  algebra of admissible properties given by a partition of the points, and the filter of positive
  properties generated by a core); hand-coded intensions of Anderson's and Scott's definitions.
- `nd.py`: syntax, substitution, definition unfolding, and a line-by-line checker for labelled natural
  deduction in Lemmon style (dependency sets), plus the LaTeX typesetter for derivations.
- `derivations.py`: the axioms, definitions and the two derivations of Part B, written as data.
- `sem.py`: direct evaluation of the formulas of `derivations.py` in the models of `models.py`, used as an
  independent cross-check of Part A against the syntax of Part B.
- `generate.py`: enumerates the models of Part A up to isomorphism, checks them, checks the derivations,
  cross-checks, and writes `models_sym.tex`, `models_nonsym.tex`, `modelcounts.tex`, `modelstats.tex`,
  `deriv_af.tex`, `deriv_scott.tex`. It exits with an error if any check fails.

Frame identifiers F<n>.<k> are those of the listing of REL-01-b (same enumeration and ordering).
