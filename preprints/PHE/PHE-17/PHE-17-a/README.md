# PHE-17-a · The Recurrence of the Crisis

- **Family:** PHE-17 — Is the life-world prior to science?
- **Type:** principal
- **Discipline:** Phenomenology
- **Estimated length:** 12 pages
- **Formalized:** no

## Main result

Yes. Science is a founded extension of the life-world that tends to forget its foundation; the crisis Husserl diagnosed is recurrent rather than unique.
