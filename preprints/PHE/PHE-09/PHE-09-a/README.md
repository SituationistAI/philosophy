- **Family:** PHE-09 — Is the look of the other essentially conflictual?

- **Discipline:** Phenomenology
- **Estimated length:** 5 pages

## Main result

No. Sartre's analysis of the look establishes objectification, not hostility; the conflictual reading depends on contingent social conditions.
