# MET-20-a · The Existence of Social Kinds

- **Family:** MET-20 — Do social kinds exist?
- **Type:** principal
- **Discipline:** Metaphysics
- **Estimated length:** 8 pages
- **Formalized:** no

## Main result

Yes. Social kinds exist and depend on the absence of collective rejection rather than on active acceptance; this dependence makes them no less real than natural kinds.
