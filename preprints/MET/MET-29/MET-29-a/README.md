# MET-29-a · Bundle Theory Refuted

- **Family:** MET-29 — Are objects bundles of properties?
- **Type:** principal
- **Discipline:** Metaphysics
- **Estimated length:** 2 pages
- **Formalized:** no

## Main result

No. Bundle theories that rely on absolute or relative discernibility fail as a corollary of MET-28; only a bundle theory based on weak discernibility survives.
