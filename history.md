# History

## 2026-10-07 — Corrections following initial release

Three manuscripts have been withdrawn. All three relied on Lemma 3.2 of MET-20-a, which states the persistence condition for institutions as continued collective acceptance. The argument requires the weaker condition of absence of collective rejection. The two conditions are not equivalent: an institution that no one actively endorses but no one rejects persists under the second and not under the first. Neither affected family was formalized.

Withdrawn:

- MET-20-b · Money, Borders and Collective Acceptance
- MET-20-c · The Persistence Conditions of Institutions
- POL-07-b · From Protective Associations to the State

Fourteen manuscripts have been revised (proof repairs and corrected statements). Four of these issues were identified during formalization.

| Id | Title | Found during formalization | Change |
